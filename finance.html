The Refunding Clause Register

Pursuant to Article Nine, Clause 19.edu.rfc of the Constitution, twenty per centum (20%) of all first-party profits and revenue generated from school-sponsored events or paid meal services are collected to ensure the continued operation of the Government.

Writ of Tuition

Per 19.edu.wot, colleges may request a Writ of Tuition if enrollment exceeds funding. Current flat rate maximum is 30% of per-capita funding.
